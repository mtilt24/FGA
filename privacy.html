Privacy Policy.

What we collect, what we do not, and how to reach us.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

The short version: we ask for your email so we can send you the newsletter. We do not track you, we do not use advertising cookies, and we never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who we are

FEEL Good Always is the podcast and newsletter of Becky Bradley, published at feelgoodalways.org. If you have a question about anything on this page, email Becky@feelgoodalways.org.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form yourself:

  • Your email address, when you sign up for the newsletter.
  • Your first and last name, if you sign up through one of the forms that asks for them. The newsletter page and the popup ask for your email only.
  • Whatever you write to us, if you email Becky directly.

That is the whole list. We do not ask for your address, your phone number, or your date of birth, and there is no account to create.

What we do not do

  • We do not run Google Analytics or any other analytics tool. We cannot see which pages you visited.
  • We do not use advertising pixels, retargeting, or trackers from Facebook, Google, or anyone else.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with anyone for their own marketing.
  • We do not build a profile of you or try to work out who you are.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies, which is why you have never seen a cookie banner here.

There is one small exception worth naming. When the newsletter popup appears, your browser saves a single item called fga_newsletter_popup_seen in its local storage. Its only job is to remember that you have already seen the popup so it never interrupts you twice. It is not a cookie, it is never sent to us or anyone else, it contains nothing about you, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser data.

What we do with your email

We send you the newsletter. That is it. You can unsubscribe from the bottom of any email we send, and doing so removes you from the list straight away.

We keep your email on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Services we rely on

Running a website means a handful of other companies are involved. Each one is used for one narrow job:

  • Mailchimp stores the newsletter list and sends the emails. Your email address lives there.
  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, its servers keep short-lived request logs that include IP addresses. We do not use those logs to identify or track visitors.
  • Cloudinary serves the images.
  • Buzzsprout hosts the podcast and its player.
  • rss.app powers the feed embeds on the homepage.
  • YouTube hosts the retreat videos, embedded in privacy-enhanced mode so nothing is set unless you press play.
  • Stripe handles retreat payments.

Retreat bookings

Payments for retreat rooms go through Stripe's own secure checkout on Stripe's website. Your card details are entered there, not here, and we never see or store them. Stripe tells us who paid and for what, nothing more.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter. You can also email Becky@feelgoodalways.org to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted, and we will take care of it. You do not need to give a reason, and depending on where you live you may have these rights by law regardless.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will remove them.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect ever changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top tells you when it was last revised.

Questions about any of this? Email Becky at Becky@feelgoodalways.org.
