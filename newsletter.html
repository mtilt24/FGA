Hi, I'm Becky.

I'm a mom, a seeker, a teacher, a student learning from all that this life is here to teach me, and most of all, I'm a mother on a mission.

Since my daughter Keira's transition and the soul crushing grief that came along with that, I have been opened up to be able to see, hear, and feel many things. I have had profound experiences, so many signs, beyond simple synchronicities. I have been shown things that I would have never believed could be possible in the physical world.

Keira has connected in this way with many others too, and it is in talking to others about these experiences that I am shown this is possible for not only me, but for you too.

Separation is an illusion. Anything is possible. Becky Bradley
